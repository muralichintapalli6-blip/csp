Frequently Asked Questions

How do students register on this portal?
Students do not need separate accounts to browse study materials or watch lesson streams. They can browse directly! If a student wants their scores to be recorded in performance logs, they can log in using credentials provided by their class teachers.
Who approves the educational files and videos?
To maintain structural quality standards, all quizzes, lesson notes, and videos uploaded by teachers enter a "Pending review" queue. The Principal moderates and approves files before they become visible on the public Student Portal.
Can teachers log in using their own credentials?
Yes. The Principal creates accounts for teachers and assigns them specific classes and subjects. Teachers can log in using their assigned emails and passwords to access their customizable educator dashboard.

Government & Meritorious Scholarships

National Means-Cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS)

Offered by the Ministry of Education for Class 8 students with family income under ₹3.5 Lakhs. Covers ₹12,000 yearly for study support till Class 12.

National Talent Search Examination (NTSE)

A prestigious national-level exam for Class 10 students. Qualified scholars receive monthly stipends for higher secondary, undergraduate, and postgraduate programs.

State Welfare & Post-Matric Scholarships

Provides complete fee reimbursement and residential allowances for socially backward communities enrolling in Intermediate, ITI, and Polytechnic colleges.
